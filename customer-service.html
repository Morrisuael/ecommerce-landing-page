Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Online order placement is currently unavailable. Contact customer support for purchase enquiries.

Will my cart keep its items?

Your cart is saved in this browser, so it remains available on this device. You can update quantities, remove individual products, or clear the cart from the checkout page.

How can I track my delivery?

Contact customer support with your order number for delivery assistance.

What if my city is missing from the checkout list?

Choose the manual-entry option to enter your location. Country and state names are sent to CountriesNow to retrieve suggestions; your contact and street-address details are not sent to that service.

Privacy information

Checkout details are not transmitted or saved. The cart is stored in this browser’s local storage.

Selecting a country or state requests location suggestions from CountriesNow. Only the selected country and state values are sent for that lookup; the service’s own privacy practices apply.

E-waste information

Dispose of electronics and batteries through an authorized e-waste collection or recycling program in your area, or contact the device manufacturer or local authority for current guidance.

Before recycling a device, remove personal data and follow the manufacturer’s instructions for battery handling. Do not place electronics or batteries in household waste where local rules prohibit it.
